# Widget Forge

**Widget Forge** is an advanced, highly customizable stat bar and widget management suite for World of Warcraft. It empowers you to track essential character data, party cooldowns, and specific game mechanics exactly where and how you want them on your screen, all while keeping your UI clean and minimalist.

## 🚀 Core Features

- **Customizable Stat Bars:** Track real-time character data such as Health, Armor, Run Speed, and Primary Stats (Strength, Agility, Stamina, Intellect).
- **Dynamic Auto-Resizing:** Bar backgrounds automatically expand or shrink to perfectly fit their content, ensuring a polished look at all times.
- **Precision Layout Control:** Fine-tune spacing between elements (including negative spacing for dense layouts) and use visual dividers (lines or text characters) to organize your data.
- **Real-Time Configuration:** Adjust settings through a modern, slide-in collapsible ("accordion") panel, and see your changes applied instantly on the screen without reloading.

## 🧩 Comprehensive Widget Library

Widget Forge includes a variety of specialized, purpose-built widgets tailored for different aspects of gameplay. 

### 1. Dragonriding Info
A dedicated heads-up display (HUD) for dragonriding. 
- **What it does:** Visually tracks your Vigor charges (as segmented boxes) and current flight speed (as a color-changing tuning bar).
- **Usage:** Perfect for maintaining momentum and resource awareness without taking your eyes off your character. It features an isolated layout mode, meaning its background and borders won't conflict with your general stat bars.

### 2. Mythic+ Dashboard
An essential tool for dungeon runners who want precise tracking.
- **What it does:** Displays the Mythic+ keystone timer, boss kill timestamps (splits), overall completion percentage, and checklist-style indicators for defeated and surviving bosses. 
- **Usage:** Use it to monitor your group's pace, analyze boss-to-boss time, and ensure you make the timer. It even has an option to automatically close your bags when a keystone is slotted!

### 3. Party Sync (CC & Interrupt Tracker)
A must-have for coordinated group content.
- **What it does:** Automatically syncs and displays the cooldowns of Crowd Control (CC) abilities and Interrupts (kicks) for everyone in your party.
- **Usage:** Place it centrally to know exactly when a party member has their interrupt ready, preventing overlapping kicks and improving dungeon survivability.

### 4. Cursor Ring
Never lose track of your mouse pointer again.
- **What it does:** Renders a customizable, unobtrusive visual ring directly around your mouse cursor.
- **Usage:** Turn it on with the "Combat Only" setting to ensure you can always find your cursor during chaotic raid encounters or massive AoE pulls, without cluttering the screen while resting in a city.

### 5. Target Castbar & Skill Stream
Enhanced visibility for enemy actions.
- **What it does:** Provides a highly visible, dedicated castbar for your current target and streams important incoming skills.
- **Usage:** Position it near the center of your screen to ensure you never miss a critical interrupt or defensive window against enemy casts.

### 6. Retirement Home
For players who prefer a minimalist or conditional UI.
- **What it does:** A specialized widget container designed to only appear under specific conditions, utilizing "Combat Only" or "Instance Only" visibility toggles.
- **Usage:** Create a setup that gives you all the data you need while fighting a boss, but makes it completely vanish when you are exploring the open world or AFK in a capital city.

### 7. Vault Eye
- **What it does:** A compact widget that tracks your Great Vault weekly progress.
- **Usage:** Quickly glance at your screen to see how many raid bosses, Mythic+ dungeons, or PvP activities you need to complete for your next vault slot without having to open multiple menus.

### 8. Custom Code Sandbox
For advanced users who want absolute freedom.
- **What it does:** Allows you to input your own custom Lua code snippets to track and display virtually anything in the game.
- **Usage:** Want to track a very specific, obscure buff? Or calculate a unique stat ratio? Write a Lua snippet, and this widget will display the result in real-time. You can even toggle text drop shadows for pixel-perfect styling.

## 🎮 How to Use (Commands & Configuration)

**Chat Commands:**
- `/wf` - Toggles the Widget Forge configuration panel.

**Configuring Your Interface:**
1. **Open the Menu:** Type `/wf` to open the Slide-In Panel. You'll navigate through collapsible categories like "System", "Stats", and "Utils".
2. **Toggle Elements:** Click on any stat or widget button (e.g., "Max HP", "Mythic+ Tracker") to activate it. Active elements are highlighted in blue. Click again to remove them.
3. **Positioning:** Every active widget or stat block has a "drag handle". Simply click and drag these handles to position the elements exactly where you want them on your screen.
4. **Customizing the Look:** Navigate to the appearance settings to tweak fonts, background colors, borders, and transparency. Use the sliders to adjust spacing (down to -10 for ultra-compact looks) and watch the UI update in real-time.
